# Disclaimer

## Not Medical Advice

MedicalDialog is a template and workflow tool designed to help you prepare for medical appointments and better understand your health records. It is not a medical product, and nothing produced by using these templates — including any output from AI assistants — constitutes medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions. Do not delay or avoid seeking professional medical care based on anything an AI assistant tells you.

## AI Limitations

AI assistants can make mistakes. They can misinterpret data, produce plausible-sounding but incorrect information, and are not aware of your full medical context. Output from AI assistants should always be reviewed critically and discussed with your doctor, not acted on directly.

AI assistants are not a substitute for a physician, nurse, pharmacist, or any other licensed healthcare provider.

## Emergency Situations

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, stop and call emergency services immediately (911 in the US, 112 in Europe, or your local emergency number). Do not consult an AI assistant in an emergency.
